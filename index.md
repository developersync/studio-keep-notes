# Game pass price vs what actually lands in your balance

The number you type into a game pass price is never the number that lands in your balance. This page is a plain table to check before you set a price. Nothing fancy.

## The rule

When a player buys a game pass or a developer product, Roblox keeps a 30% marketplace fee and you get 70%. Your share is a whole number of Robux, so anything after the decimal is dropped. That is why cheap passes look worse than 70%.

A 5 Robux pass: 5 × 0.7 = 3.5, and you get **3**. That is 60%, not 70%.

## Working backwards from what you want to keep

This is usually the more useful direction. Divide what you want by 0.7 and round **up**. Then check one below it, because rounding down your share can cost you a single Robux.

| Want to keep (R$) | Set the price to | One lower gives you |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 50 | 72 | 49 |
| 100 | 143 | 99 |
| 250 | 358 | 249 |
| 500 | 715 | 499 |
| 1,000 | 1,429 | 999 |
| 5,000 | 7,143 | 4,999 |

So if you need 500 Robux per sale, 714 is one short. 715 is the price.

If a price you want is not in either table, there is a calculator that shows the amount [after Roblox tax](https://rbxcalc.net/) for any pass price, and it does the reverse sum too.

## What that is worth in cash

If you cash out through the Developer Exchange (DevEx), the standard rate is $0.0038 USD per Earned Robux, which is $114 for the 30,000 Earned Robux minimum. Robux earned before 5 September 2025 cash out at the old $0.0035 rate, and those older Robux go first. Since 8 June 2026 there is also a higher $0.0054 rate for some Earned Robux from age-checked US players aged 18+ in eligible games.

Rough example at the standard rate: a 500 Robux pass that sells 100 times earns 35,000 Robux, which is about $133 USD before anything your bank does with it.

DevEx also needs you to be at least 13, have a verified email, and have a tax form on file, and you can complete one cash out per calendar month. The full list is on the [Creator Hub DevEx page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-exchange).

## Easy things to miss

- Robux players buy with real money are not Earned Robux and cannot be cashed out.
- Avatar items in the Marketplace use a different revenue share, so do not use this table for UGC.
- Changing a pass price later only affects new sales. Re-check the table before a price drop event.
